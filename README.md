# Abuse actor — phishing takedown coordinator

Canonical repository: `etzhayyim/actor-abuse`.

Abuse is an organ of the Tamaki artificial organism. It gathers public
infrastructure evidence and prepares phishing or impersonation takedown drafts;
it is not an independent enforcement authority and does not replace Tamaki's
governance boundary.

Every outward dispatch remains proposal-only until explicitly authorized by the
configured human or scoped caller. The actor must never bypass the approval,
rate-limit, or append-only audit requirements in `actor-manifest.jsonld` and
`CLAUDE.md`.

The stable public actor identity remains `did:web:abuse.etzhayyim.com`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the runtime, XRPC, and governance contracts.
